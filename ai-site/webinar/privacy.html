Webinar privacy

This notice covers the October 13, 2026 AI webinar and related setup inquiries to Mark Andrew.

Registration and event access

Registration takes place on Zoom. Zoom collects the details you provide in its registration form, including your name and email address, and makes registration information available to the event host. Those details are used to administer the webinar and provide event access. Zoom’s own privacy information is available from its registration page.

Webinar registration does not subscribe you to ongoing AI marketing emails or photography campaigns. This website does not collect your webinar registration details in a separate form.

Setup inquiries

If you email Mark about a setup, your message and contact details are received in Mark’s Gmail account and used to review and respond to your inquiry. The website’s email button opens your email app; it does not send or save an inquiry itself. Please do not include passwords, private customer information, or other sensitive records.

Website visits

The webinar pages use locally hosted images and fonts. They do not add advertising pixels, third-party embeds, a mailing-list form, or browser tracking scripts. The website host, Vercel, may process standard request information needed to provide and protect the site.

Questions or removal requests

Email markandrewpics@gmail.com about your webinar information or setup inquiry. Identify the email address used for registration so Mark can locate the relevant record. Information held independently by Zoom or your email provider is also subject to that provider’s policies.
