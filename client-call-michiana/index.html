Common hesitations

Yes, you can. Do this.

"Where is the studio?"
We photograph women from Granger, South Bend, Mishawaka, and across the Michiana area. We'll choose the studio location together during your planning call based on the vision for your session, and share the exact address before your shoot.
"Why are you offering complimentary sessions?"
We're updating our studio's viewing room and need women to photograph for new sample albums, wall prints, and other products to be featured in studio. In exchange for being featured, your session is on us.
"How long will the session last?"
This is a full-day event. We start in the morning, the photoshoot wraps around lunchtime, then you'll come back that afternoon to see your edited, retouched pictures the same day — and we send you on your way with your newfound confidence.
"Can my partner be photographed too?"
Yes — we love photographing couples. They're welcome to join your shoot at no extra cost.
"Can I bring a friend?"
Yes — your friend is welcome to stay while you get ready. Aletheia stays during the entire shoot to assist with wardrobe and touchups.
"Are hair and makeup included?"
No. Hair and makeup are not included in the complimentary session. We'll go over how to prepare during your planning call.
"Is there a booking retainer?"
Yes. If selected, your $500 session fee is waived. A $99 booking retainer reserves your date and is credited toward any photographs or products you choose to purchase. Photographs and products are not included in the complimentary session.
"Do I have to buy any photos?"
No. Purchasing photographs or products is optional. The $99 booking retainer is still required to reserve your session. You'll see 100+ edited images at your viewing and choose what (if anything) you want — but fair warning: you're going to love how you look in your pictures.
"What if I want to buy keepsakes?"
The average client spends about $1,000, and we offer flexible payment plans on anything you take home.
"I'm not photogenic."
Almost everyone says this. It's not true — it's just that you've never been photographed by someone who's used to photographing every figure, every age, every personality. Mark has spent a decade in studio with more than 850 women across every body type and every season of life, and he poses every shot — head, shoulders, hands, hips, breath — so you never have to "figure out what to do."
Is it awkward?
Not the way we run it. The studio is private, the team is small (Mark + Aletheia), and most women say the awkwardness they expected never showed up. Within the first 30 minutes, you'll be laughing.
"I don't know what to bring."
That's exactly what the planning call is for. We send you a full wardrobe and prep guide after you book, then walk through everything together on a 30-minute video call before the shoot.
"I don't know what to wear."
Most women say this — and almost every one ends up obsessed with what we choose together. We pick wardrobe based on your body, your colors, and the vibe you're going for. Nothing is off the rack and ill-fitting.
"I don't know how to pose."
You're not supposed to. Mark poses every shot — head, shoulders, hands, hips, breath — frame by frame. You never have to "figure out what to do." That's his entire job.
