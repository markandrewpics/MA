Before you hit the road.

How do I get my photo?

When the show gallery is ready, find your car, tap the photo, and choose Download photo. On a phone, you can find the downloaded file in your Downloads or Files app and save it to Photos.

Do I have to enter the giveaway to download?

No. Your car-show photo is free to download. Entering the drawing is optional.

What does the winner receive?

A full photo shoot with Mark Andrew Photography and one 8×10 photographic print, with a total prize value of $758.

Can my wife or partner be in the photographs?

Absolutely. The session can include you, your car, and your wife or partner.
