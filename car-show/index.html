Hey, I’m Mark.

Photographer. Marine Corps veteran.
And the guy who wants to hear about your car.

I’m Mark Andrew Boughton, the photographer behind Mark Andrew Photography. I’ve been running my studio since 2019, and I work with people here in South Bend and throughout Michiana.

The man in these photographs is my dad. I’ve watched him work so hard to provide for our family. Seeing him own a car that means so much to him means something to me, too.

I wanted to photograph more than his Mustang. I wanted a photograph of him that I can hold onto forever—a reminder of who he is and this part of his life. I’d love to make something like that for you and your family.

Maybe it’s the car you spent years restoring. Maybe it’s a portrait with your wife beside it. You don’t need to know how to pose. I’ll guide you through the photographs so you can enjoy the experience.

If you see me at the show, come say hello. I’d love to meet you.
