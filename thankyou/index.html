05 / A few things you might be wondering

Curious is a
great place to start.

Am I booking a photoshoot right now?

No. You're choosing a consultation time. You'll discuss the experience, pricing, availability and next steps before deciding whether to book a photoshoot.

What if I've never done anything like this?

That's completely okay. Posing and expression are guided throughout the session. You can talk about your comfort level and questions on the call.

What should I have ready for the call?

Your questions, any dates you're considering, and a few ideas if you have them. If you're planning for a birthday or gift, tell Mark when you need your finished images or products.

Can I ask about pricing before I commit?

Yes. Ask about the session, image collections, optional hair and makeup, and any products you're considering so you understand the full investment.

Do my photos have to be shared online?

No. The studio's published policy says images stay private unless you specifically give permission to share them. Bring any privacy questions to your consultation.
