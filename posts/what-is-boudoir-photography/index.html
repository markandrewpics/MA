What Is Boudoir Photography Like for First-Timers in South Bend, Indiana?

Mark Andrew
12 min read

You don't show up to the studio confident. You show up to get the confidence.

Most of the clients you see in my portfolio are women who had never booked a photo shoot before. For most of them, it was their first time in front of a professional camera.

If you're in South Bend, Granger, Niles or the Notre Dame area and you're thinking about your first session, here's exactly how it works.

What Is Boudoir Photography?

Boudoir photography is intimate portraiture that shows women the way they want to be remembered. It has evolved in a lot of ways over the last couple of decades. Traditionally, it was a way for women to showcase their bodies, their sensuality, their sexuality and the human form, often as a gift for a significant other. Now more than ever, it's a way to fall in love with the skin you're in.

My clients in South Bend, Granger, Niles and the Notre Dame area book me because my style is timeless, elegant and tasteful, but still sexy enough to make your partner blush. These are images you'll cherish forever. I want you to love how you look in them today, and I want you to still love them in 10, 20, 30, 40 and 50 years.

Your Studio Day, From Arrival to Same-Day Reveal

The best part of working with me in the studio: you see your edited, retouched pictures the same day we take them.

Morning arrival. Most clients like to arrive first thing, around 9 or 10 AM. The first thing we do is go over wardrobe one last time: the sets we want to use, accessories, jewelry and all the little details that put your look together.

Hair and makeup. Then you go straight into hair and makeup with Aletheia. Whether you want something bold and dramatic or something more natural, she can do it all, and she also provides light hair styling.

The shoot. Once hair and makeup is done, you change and we start shooting. We typically do three different looks. Some clients like to start off tame. Others want to jump in with both feet.

Lunch and your reveal. After the shoot, we break for lunch. After lunch, we hop on a Zoom call to go through your edited, retouched pictures together. This is your time to pick your favorites, design your album, go over print options and tailor your package to whatever you love.

Do I Need to Know How to Pose?

No. That's the biggest misconception about boudoir. My superpower, the thing I'm absolutely best at, is helping clients look like supermodels when they've never stepped in front of a camera before.

Most of the women in my portfolio had never booked a photo shoot before, and it was their first time in front of a professional camera. I pose you head to toe. If you're good at Simon Says, you'll be great at this.

I'll also coach you through expressions: smiling with your eyes, the look, the smolder. We'll be cracking jokes the whole time, so we get beautiful smiling and laughing pictures too.

Hands matter more than most clients realize. I'll coach you into soft, elegant hands and walk you through movements that make each scene feel alive. Every little detail, I'll walk you through it.

What Should I Wear, and How Much Skin Do I Have to Show?

Every client is different. Some want to show little to no skin. Others come in wanting a full nude shoot. Whatever you're looking for, know this: you won't surprise us. After photographing nearly a thousand women, we've seen it all. We're not here to judge. We're here to give you the confidence you've always dreamed of.

On our video call, we'll talk through what you're going to wear, color schemes and the lingerie styles that suit different figures. I'll also send you my styling guide, which goes into all of this in depth and helps you find the perfect outfits.

I also created a wardrobe concierge service to help you put together the perfect looks, with everything from shoes and jewelry to robes, cover-ups, lingerie and every little detail of your vision. It's a free tool you can use whether you work with me or with another photographer.

Hair and Makeup With Aletheia

Aletheia's job is to help you look and feel your best, which is why the hair and makeup chair is the first stop of the day. Bold, dramatic or natural, she can do it all.

She also stays for the entire shoot for makeup touch-ups and wardrobe help, whether that's a strap, a garter belt, a bra or anything else.

Hair and makeup is paid directly to Aletheia. She charges $200 for hair, makeup and lashes.

Aletheia, hair and makeup: https://www.instagram.com/glambyaletheia/

Who's in the Studio, and Who Sees My Pictures?

The only people in the studio during your shoot are me, Aletheia and you. Our policy is simple: if you're not being photographed, you don't stay for the shoot. We want the space to feel free, safe and completely judgment-free, so you can be your true, authentic self.

Who sees your pictures is completely up to you. If you want them completely private, that's your choice. If you want to share them with the world, we'd love to feature them on our social media, but that's your choice too.

Choosing Your Images and Artwork

After lunch, we jump on Zoom and go through all of your edited, retouched images. This is when you choose the artwork you love most: an album, prints for the wall, or images for your phone. It's completely up to you.

Images are purchased separately from your booking retainer, and there's no minimum purchase. You're not locked into a package. We want you to take home what you love, and we offer flexible payment plans on anything you take home.

Our albums and other products are handcrafted, some in Portugal and some here in the United States, so they generally take 3 to 6 weeks to arrive at your door.

Should You Book? Honest Advice for Women in Michiana

Sometimes a leap of faith is what you need, even when you don't feel perfectly ready.

Maybe you're not sure you'll love the images. Maybe you think you need to lose weight first. Maybe you feel like you don't have anyone to do the shoot for. Take the leap anyway. You don't show up to the studio confident. You show up to get the confidence. It's part of the experience.

You don't need anyone to book this for. This whole experience was designed with you in mind: not him, not her, not them. We believe boudoir photography is for every figure, including yours, and that you deserve to fall in love with yourself exactly as you are right now.

We've had many clients do a "before" shoot right before starting a workout program. When they came back for their "after" shoot, they told me they were still absolutely in love with their before pictures. Those images showed a side of them they don't get to see now that they're fit: the sparkle in their eye, the hope of reaching their goals, and the tenacity and resilience they had.

So my advice is: don't wait. One reason to wait is if you aren't financially ready. My average client spends between $1,000 and $2,000 on their pictures, and some spend several thousand. What I'd hate is for you to come in, fall in love with everything and then be unable to afford what you love. If money is what's holding you back, that's worth considering.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need modeling experience?

No. Most of my clients have never been in front of a professional camera before. I pose you head to toe and coach you through expressions, hands and movement.

How much skin do I have to show?

That's up to you. Some clients show little to no skin; others do a full nude shoot. We plan your wardrobe and coverage together on our video call.

When do I see my pictures?

The same day. After the shoot we break for lunch, then go through your edited, retouched images together on a Zoom call.

Who is in the studio during my shoot?

Only me, Aletheia and you. If you're not being photographed, you don't stay for the shoot.

Who sees my pictures?

Only who you choose. You can keep them completely private, or let us share them on our social media.

Is there a minimum purchase?

No. Images are purchased separately from your booking retainer, there's no package you're locked into, and we offer flexible payment plans. Albums and other handcrafted products generally take 3 to 6 weeks to arrive.
