the honest part

Everybody's feed looks exactly the same now.

Scroll your own industry's hashtag for thirty seconds. Same generated headshot. Same stock photo of a laptop and a latte. Same slightly-too-smooth face that doesn't quite exist.

It's cheap, it's instant, and it makes you invisible. When every profile in the feed was made by the same three tools, none of them mean anything.

Here's the thing nobody wants to say out loud: people don't hire a logo. They hire a person they've already decided they can trust — usually before they've read a single word you wrote. AI can render a face. It can't render the part your best clients actually respond to, which is that you're real, you showed up, and you cared enough to be photographed as yourself.

Your customers deserve to know you. The actual you — not a rendering of somebody who looks a bit like you.

questions

The things people actually ask.

I'm not photogenic. I hate photos of myself.
Almost everyone says this on the brand call, and almost everyone is wrong about why. "Not photogenic" is usually just bad light, no direction, and a phone held at a terrible angle. I pose you head to toe — chin, hands, shoulders, weight, every angle — and I light you deliberately. You don't need modeling experience. You need someone whose job it is to know what to do next.
What do I wear?
We plan it together on the brand call, before you go shopping. Generally: one look that's how your best clients see you at work, one that's elevated a notch above that, and one that's genuinely you off the clock. Bring the real props too — your keys, your cards, your tools, your product. Those details are what stop the photos from looking generic.
How many photos do I get, and when?
You'll see 130 to 160 fully edited images at your reveal the same afternoon. What you take home depends on the collection you choose — digitals, wall art, metal prints, folio boxes, and the acrylic-cover albums the studio is known for. Nothing takes two to four weeks like it does most places.
Can I use these in ads, on my website, and on social?
Yes — commercial usage is the entire point of a branding session, and we sort out exactly what you need on the call. That's the main difference between this and a portrait session: these images are built to go to work for your business.
Do you really not use AI?
Correct. No generated faces, no generated backgrounds, no AI "enhancement" quietly reshaping you into someone else. Everything you'll see is light, a lens, and retouching that stays honest — the same person who walked in that morning.
Where are you, and do you travel?
Two studios. In Michiana I shoot South Bend, Mishawaka, Granger, Elkhart and Goshen, plus Niles, Edwardsburg and Berrien Springs across the Michigan line. In the Lowcountry it's Beaufort, Bluffton, Hilton Head, Savannah and Charleston. On-location work in your own space is very doable — just mention it on the call.
What does it cost?
It's a real investment, and usually more than people expect before they see what a full day actually produces. I'd rather give you a straight number on the call once I know what you need than post a misleading "starting at" price here. Affirm payment plans are available.
Can you do our whole team?
Yes. Team days work well — matched lighting and styling across everybody so the website finally looks like one company instead of eleven different phone cameras. Bring it up on the call and we'll build the day around your headcount.
