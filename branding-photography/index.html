questions

The things people actually ask.

I'm not photogenic. I hate photos of myself.
Almost everyone says this, and almost everyone is wrong about why. "Not photogenic" is usually bad light, no direction and a phone held at a terrible angle — not your face. I pose you head to toe and light you deliberately. You don't need experience in front of a camera. You need someone whose entire job is knowing what should happen next.
What do I wear?
You don't have to solve this one — it's the part most people dread and the part I take off you. After the brand call I come back with a plan: what to wear, which colours sit right against your skin and your brand, what to bring. Generally one look that's how your best clients already see you, one a notch above that, and one that's genuinely you off the clock. If you'd rather not shop at all, say so and we'll build it from what you already own.
How many photos do I get, and when?
You'll see 150+ finished images the same day, while the shoot is still fresh in your head. What you take home depends on the collection you choose — digitals, wall art, metal prints, folio boxes and albums. Nothing sits in a queue for a month.
Can I use these in ads, on my website, and on social?
Yes — commercial usage is the entire point of a branding session, and we sort out exactly what you need on the call. That's the main difference between this and a portrait session: these images are built to go to work for your business.
Do you really not use AI?
Correct. No generated faces, no generated backgrounds, no AI "enhancement" quietly reshaping you into someone else. Everything you'll see is light, a lens, and retouching that stays honest — the same person who walked in that morning.
Where are you, and do you travel?
Short answer: yes, and it's never once been the hard part. I have two studios — Michiana (South Bend, Mishawaka, Granger, Elkhart, Goshen, plus Niles, Edwardsburg and Berrien Springs over the Michigan line) and the Lowcountry (Beaufort, Bluffton, Hilton Head, Savannah, Charleston). Beyond that it goes both ways. Some clients fly in and make a weekend of it. Some line their shoot up with a city I'm already going to be in. And some of my favourite work on this page happened because I got on a plane — the Gateway Arch at midnight, a newsstand in front of the Flatiron. If your business lives somewhere else, say so on the brand call. We'll make the geography work; we always do.
What does it cost?
It's a real investment, and usually more than people expect — until they see what they walk out with. I'd rather give you a straight number on the call, once I know what you actually need, than post a misleading "starting at" price here. Affirm payment plans are available.
Can you do our whole team?
Yes. Team days work well — matched lighting and styling across everybody so the website finally looks like one company instead of eleven different phone cameras. Bring it up on the call and we'll build the day around your headcount.
